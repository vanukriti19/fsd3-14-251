ChatGPT is an artificial intelligence chatbot developed by OpenAI. It is designed to understand and generate human-like text, allowing users to interact with it through natural language conversations. ChatGPT can answer questions, explain concepts, write content, generate code, assist with learning, summarize information, and help with a wide variety of tasks.

The model is based on large language model technology and has been trained on a mixture of publicly available data, licensed data, and data created by human trainers. It uses patterns learned during training to predict and generate relevant responses to user prompts.

People use ChatGPT for many purposes, including education, software development, content creation, business communication, research, brainstorming, language translation, and productivity enhancement. It can adapt its responses to different levels of expertise, making it useful for both beginners and experienced professionals.

ChatGPT is available through web and mobile applications, as well as APIs that allow developers to integrate AI-powered conversational capabilities into their own products and services. Newer versions support multimodal interactions, enabling users to work with text, images, files, and other forms of input.

While ChatGPT can provide helpful information and assistance, it may occasionally generate incorrect or outdated information. Users should verify important facts, especially when making decisions related to health, finance, law, or other high-stakes domains.

Overall, ChatGPT serves as a versatile AI assistant that helps users learn, create, solve problems, and complete tasks more efficiently through conversational interaction.
